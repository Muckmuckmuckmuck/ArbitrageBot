**NO TRADING LIMITS** on standard accounts for **TRADING VOLUME**! ✅

## 📋 WHAT'S LIMITED VS WHAT'S NOT

### ✅ **NO LIMITS** (Standard Account):

1. **Trading Volume** ✅
   - Trade as much as you want per day
   - No monthly trading limits
   - $100/day, $10k/day, $100k/day - all fine
   - **Your bot can trade unlimited** ✅

2. **Number of Trades** ✅
   - Unlimited trades per day
   - Your bot can make 100+ trades/day
   - No restrictions

3. **API Calls** ✅
   - Subject to rate limits (to prevent abuse)
   - But rate limits are generous (10 req/sec)
   - Your bot stays well under these (6.7 req/sec)

### ⚠️  **LIMITS THAT DO EXIST** (Standard Account):

1. **Withdrawal Limits** 💰
   - **Pionex.US Standard**: ~$50,000/day withdrawal limit
   - **Coinbase Pro Standard**: $50,000-250,000/day (depends on verification level)
   - Note: This is **withdrawal to bank/external wallet**, NOT trading

2. **Deposit Limits** 💰
   - Usually quite high ($100k+/day)
   - Increases with account verification level
   - Rarely an issue
